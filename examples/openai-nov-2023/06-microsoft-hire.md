# Microsoft announces Altman and Brockman will lead new AI group

> Demo content — paraphrased from public statements by Microsoft chief executive Satya Nadella on 20 November 2023.

Microsoft chief executive Satya Nadella announced today that Sam Altman and Greg Brockman, together with colleagues, will join Microsoft to lead a new advanced AI research group. Mr Altman will serve as chief executive of the new group, with Mr Brockman as president.

In a public statement, Mr Nadella said Microsoft remained "extremely committed" to its partnership with OpenAI and had confidence in its product roadmap, the technology it had been building together, and its strong commercial relationship. He added that Microsoft was looking forward to getting to know Emmett Shear, the newly designated interim chief executive at OpenAI, and the OpenAI leadership team, and to working with them.

Microsoft did not disclose the structure or size of the new subsidiary, but Mr Nadella said the company would move quickly to provide Altman, Brockman, and any colleagues joining them with the resources required to continue their work. He emphasised that Microsoft's investment in OpenAI was unaffected and that the company's product integrations with OpenAI's models — including in Azure, Copilot, and Bing — would continue without disruption.

The announcement followed a weekend of intense negotiations during which Altman's potential return to OpenAI had been actively explored. Mr Nadella said Microsoft had been ready for several possible outcomes, including Altman's reinstatement.

In a subsequent post on X, Sam Altman confirmed his next chapter would be at Microsoft, writing that the mission continues.
