# Emmett Shear named interim CEO of OpenAI

> Demo content — paraphrased from Emmett Shear's public statement on 20 November 2023.

In a post on X early Monday, Emmett Shear announced that he had accepted the role of interim chief executive at OpenAI. Mr Shear, a co-founder and former chief executive of the live streaming service Twitch, said he had taken the job because of the unique importance of OpenAI's mission and his belief in its team.

Mr Shear stated that the prior interim arrangement under which Mira Murati had been named CEO on Friday was no longer in effect, and that he was now the company's interim chief executive while the board continued its search for a permanent leader.

He acknowledged that the board's process had drawn substantial criticism. He committed to commissioning an independent investigation into the events leading to Sam Altman's removal, to be conducted by an external firm, with its findings to be made public. He also said he would hire significantly more senior leaders, reform the company's management practices where warranted, and act swiftly to address employee concerns.

Mr Shear noted that he would have walked away from any offer that he believed would meaningfully slow OpenAI's commercial business. He said he intended to bring stability to the company within thirty days and ensure that customers and partners could continue to depend on OpenAI's products.

By later that day, however, reporting indicated that talks between the board and Altman had resumed and that a deal to restore Altman to the chief executive role was being actively negotiated.
