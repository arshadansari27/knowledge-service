# OpenAI employees demand board's resignation

> Demo content — paraphrased from the openly circulated employee letter dated 20 November 2023.

In an open letter addressed to the board of directors of OpenAI, Inc., more than seven hundred employees of the company — believed at the time of signing to represent roughly ninety percent of the workforce — wrote that the board had jeopardised the company's work and that they were prepared to resign en masse unless the directors stepped down and reinstated Sam Altman as chief executive.

The letter, which was signed by an expanding list of researchers, engineers, and operational staff over the course of Monday, made three core assertions. First, that the board's stated rationale for removing Altman — a loss of confidence following his alleged failure to be "consistently candid" — had not been substantiated to employees in any meaningful way. Second, that the board's process had been "deeply flawed", involving no consultation with leadership outside of the directors themselves. Third, that Microsoft, the company's largest commercial partner, had offered to place all OpenAI employees in equivalent roles at a newly announced AI subsidiary.

Signatories included Mira Murati, the interim chief executive named by the board on Friday, and Ilya Sutskever, the chief scientist and a sitting board member who had previously voted in favour of Altman's removal.

The letter gave the board no deadline but stated that signatories would begin to leave for Microsoft's new subsidiary if the board did not resign and reinstate Altman.

By Tuesday morning the signatory count had reportedly crossed seven hundred.
