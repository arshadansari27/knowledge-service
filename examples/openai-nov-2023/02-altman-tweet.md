# Sam Altman reacts to OpenAI departure

> Demo content — paraphrased from Sam Altman's first public response on 17 November 2023.

In a short post on X, Sam Altman acknowledged his departure from OpenAI hours after the board's announcement. He described his time at the company as transformative for him personally and for the field of AI more broadly, and thanked the team he had worked with.

He said he would have more to say about what comes next at a later time.

Altman did not directly contest the board's framing of the decision. He noted that he loved his time at OpenAI and that the people there were "the best in the world".

The post was the only public statement from Altman on the day of his removal. By the following morning, intense speculation about both his next move and the board's reasoning had begun to circulate through industry channels.
