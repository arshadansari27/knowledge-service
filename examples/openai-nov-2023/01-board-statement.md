# OpenAI announces leadership transition

> Demo content — paraphrased from the publicly released OpenAI board statement of 17 November 2023.

The board of directors of OpenAI, Inc., the 501(c)(3) parent of OpenAI's for-profit subsidiaries, today announced a leadership transition. Sam Altman is departing as Chief Executive Officer and leaving the board. Mira Murati, the company's Chief Technology Officer, will serve as interim CEO with immediate effect.

The board's review, conducted over the preceding weeks, concluded that Mr Altman was "not consistently candid in his communications" with directors, hindering its ability to exercise its responsibilities. The board no longer has confidence in his continued ability to lead OpenAI.

Greg Brockman is stepping down as chairman of the board but will remain at the company in his role as president, reporting to the new CEO.

The remaining members of the board are Adam D'Angelo, Tasha McCauley, Helen Toner, and OpenAI co-founder and chief scientist Ilya Sutskever.

OpenAI's mission — to ensure that artificial general intelligence benefits all of humanity — is unchanged by today's transition. The board reaffirms its commitment to that mission and to OpenAI's charter.

Ms Murati has been with OpenAI for more than five years and has played a central role in the development of the company's research products, including ChatGPT and DALL·E. The board has full confidence in her ability to lead through this period.

A search for a permanent CEO will begin immediately.
