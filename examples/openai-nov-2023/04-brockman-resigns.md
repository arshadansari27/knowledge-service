# Greg Brockman announces resignation from OpenAI

> Demo content — paraphrased from Greg Brockman's public statement on the evening of 17 November 2023.

In a long post on X, Greg Brockman said he was leaving OpenAI. Although the board had announced earlier in the day that he would remain at the company as president after being removed as chairman, Brockman wrote that based on the events of the day he had decided to step down.

Brockman recounted being informed of Sam Altman's firing only minutes before it was made public, and said neither he nor Altman had been given any opportunity to address whatever concerns the board had raised in advance. He thanked the OpenAI team and said he was proud of the work the company had done.

He did not say where he would go next, only that he was "super proud" of what the OpenAI team had built and that he looked forward to "finding out what is next".

Reports overnight indicated that several other senior researchers at OpenAI had also tendered their resignations, citing both the manner of Altman's removal and the board's refusal to provide a clearer explanation of its decision.
