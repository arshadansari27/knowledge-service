# Sam Altman returns as CEO of OpenAI under restructured board

> Demo content — paraphrased from the OpenAI announcement of 22 November 2023.

OpenAI announced today that it has reached an agreement in principle for Sam Altman to return as chief executive, and for the board of directors to be substantially reconstituted. Greg Brockman will return as president.

The new initial board will consist of Bret Taylor, the former co-chief executive of Salesforce, serving as chair, alongside Lawrence Summers, the former United States Treasury Secretary, and Adam D'Angelo, the chief executive of Quora, who remains from the prior board.

The new board will undertake a more thorough review of the events leading up to Altman's removal on 17 November and will work to expand the directorate further. The previous independent directors — Helen Toner and Tasha McCauley — are leaving the board as part of the agreement. Ilya Sutskever, the chief scientist and co-founder, will also leave the board, though OpenAI said it was still discussing his ongoing role at the company.

Mira Murati, who had served as interim chief executive between Friday and Sunday, will resume her role as chief technology officer. Emmett Shear, who had been named interim chief executive on Sunday evening, returns to his work outside the company. In a brief statement, Mr Shear said he was "deeply pleased" with the outcome and grateful to have played his part.

Mr Altman, in his own statement, said he was looking forward to returning to OpenAI and to building on the company's relationship with Microsoft. Satya Nadella, Microsoft's chief executive, said Microsoft was "encouraged" by the changes to OpenAI's board and saw them as a first essential step on a path to more stable, well-informed, and effective governance.

OpenAI said additional details — including the final composition of the board, the scope of the independent review, and the company's longer-term governance structure — would be announced in the weeks ahead.
